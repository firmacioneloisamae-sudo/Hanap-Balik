How HanapBalik works

A clear process keeps every report organized and every claim accountable.


1. Report Submit the item details, date, location, and a helpful description.
2. Match Compare reports to identify possible lost and found matches.
3. Verify Provide ownership evidence for an administrator to review securely.
4. Return Approved claims move to release, then the item is marked returned.
